Work Rework 1.0.2 — initial priorities and direct subtask activation.

- The configured default priority is now correctly applied to starting colonists when creating a new game, instead of being reset to 3.
- The setting also applies when recruiting a prisoner as a colonist or enslaving them, when the game initializes their new work assignments.
- You can now activate a subtask directly while its parent category is disabled, without enabling the entire category first. Only the selected subtask becomes active; the others stay disabled.
- This works with clicks, the priority menu and Grouped Pawns Lists group controls.
- Parent summaries, standalone tasks, incapacities and work restrictions are preserved.
- Clarified the English and French help text.

Existing assignments in your saves are preserved. The configured default applies to new initialization events; it does not retroactively replace the values of pawns already present.

RimWorld 1.7. Harmony required. Grouped Pawns Lists remains optional.
