Work Rework 1.0.3 — default priority for group commands.

- With Grouped Pawns Lists, the first click on a group cell with mixed priorities now uses the configured default (for example 5), instead of 3.
- Collective activation in checkbox mode and its menu also use this default. The value is retained when switching back to numeric priorities.
- Subsequent clicks on a shared value keep their usual cycle. The configured maximum and pawn incapacities remain respected.
- Clarified the English and French help text.

Existing assignments are not changed automatically. RimWorld 1.7; Harmony required. Grouped Pawns Lists remains optional.
